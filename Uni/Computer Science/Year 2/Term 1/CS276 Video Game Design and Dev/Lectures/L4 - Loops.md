
## What is a loop?

A loop is a series of interactions between the player and the games systems with significant overlap between iterations.

## Core loop

Is the fundamental cycle of actions that the player must perform in order to play the game (make progress towards the goal specified by the player or devoloper)

# Situation -> Action -> Reward -/> (loop)

Player identifies what they want to achieve in the short term

Player gives an input and performs an action which causes an outcome

Player receives a result, loops backaround

# Progress / Punishment

The change that occurs in the game state as a result of ( completing / failing )some iteration of a loop.
