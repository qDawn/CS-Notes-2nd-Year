## What is "algorithms"? 

A series of steps that is used to solve a problem or complete a computation. 
Similar to a recipe or set of directions

## Why do we study them?

Solve any instance of a class of problems
Can apply to inputs of varying size
May be guaranteed to give a correct result

- Foundational : algorithms are at the heart of CS
- Coding : understanding algorithms makes you a better coder
- Motivational : algorithms are used by systems and applications 
- Preparatory : algorithms are a prerequisite for later modules
- Mathematical : algorithms develop your mathematical skills
- Career : algorithms are tested during interviews
- Philosophy : algorithmic thinking gives a different perspective
- Competition : Competitive programming relies on algorithms
- Rewarding : working with it is like a puzzle
## Examples of algorithms

### Dictionary Search
 - Start with ‘A’, look through each definition in turn till we find it
 - Open the dictionary at random and hope we find it (and repeat)
 - Jump to the starting letter (using an index), then read forwards
 - Open it half-way, then recurse on the half containing the target ( Binary Search )
